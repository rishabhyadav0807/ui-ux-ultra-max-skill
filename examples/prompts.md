# Example prompts

The skill sizes the run from the request. These show each path.

## Full run (new product, feature or redesign)

- "Design the onboarding for a savings app for first-time investors in India."
- "Redesign the checkout in this repo. It feels generic and people drop off at payment."
- "Here's a user flow for booking a home cleaner. Turn it into screens."

You get a persona, feel, principles, flow and signature moment, then a checkpoint question before any screens are built.

## Critique

- "Review this screenshot of our pricing page."
- "Critique the settings screen in `app/settings/`."

You get a verdict, a ranked "fix first" list with the principle behind each fix, and what to keep. It ends by offering a redesign.

## Light run (small, scoped fix)

- "Fix this empty state."
- "Make the primary button on this form clearer."

You get a 2–3 line persona, 1–3 principles, the fix built, and a one-line reason.

## Principle questions

- "Which UX laws apply to a plan-picker for small businesses?"
- "Is a countdown timer on this offer a dark pattern?"

You get an answer for that persona and moment, with no screens.

## For non-designers

- "I run a bakery and want an app for orders. It should look good."

The skill talks in your words, asks at most three questions about your customers, shows phone-sized screens, and explains its choices in plain language.
